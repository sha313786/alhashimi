Frequently Asked Questions

Common technical and contractual inquiries regarding DEWA clearances, project execution, and subcontracting arrangements.

What are the specific advantages of hiring a DEWA Golden Category Contractor? +
DEWA Golden Category is the highest regulatory honor awarded to contractors with an impeccable track record of safety, technical compliance, and zero defects. As a Golden Category Holder, Al Hashimi receives priority fast-track scheduling for inspections (often within 48-72 hours), expedited NOC drawing approvals, and direct access to senior DEWA engineers. This prevents the costly delays frequently encountered with non-graded contractors.
How fast can Al Hashimi obtain DEWA NOCs and electrical load approvals? +
For standard projects with complete architectural drawings, our in-house design team led by Mr. Binoji K. Varghese prepares single-line diagrams (SLD) and load schedules within 48 hours. Authority NOC approvals through DEWA’s portal typically take 3 to 5 business days, compared to the 3-4 week average for non-specialist firms.
Do you undertake complete turnkey MEP or electrical-only subcontracts? +
We offer both models. While our primary reputation is rooted in High & Low Voltage Electrical and Switchgear installation, we frequently execute complete integrated MEP packages including Extra Low Voltage (ELV/CCTV/Fire Alarm), Plumbing, Booster Pumps, and Stormwater Drainage.
What testing and commissioning procedures are performed prior to DEWA inspection? +
Prior to scheduling a DEWA inspector, our engineers conduct full pre-testing using calibrated instruments: insulation resistance (Megger test up to 1000V), earth electrode resistance (maintaining earth loop below DEWA's 1.0 Ohm threshold), polarity verification, RCD trip sensitivity, and phase rotation checks. Certified test logs are presented to DEWA on inspection day.
What warranty and Defects Liability Period (DLP) is provided upon handover? +
All Al Hashimi installations come with a standard 12-month Defects Liability Period (DLP) backed by our round-the-clock maintenance dispatch. Furthermore, major switchgear components and factory-assembled distribution boards carry manufacturers' warranties of up to 5 years.
